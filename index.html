Market Intelligence

Why Nicaragua, Why Now

The Emerald Coast is at an inflection point. Here is the data behind the opportunity you'll see firsthand.

8–12% Annual Appreciation

Coastal property prices are projected to grow 8–12% annually through 2030. Nicaragua's GDP has grown 3.5–4% consistently, with tourism and real estate leading foreign direct investment.

$400M Coastal Highway

A new highway connecting the Costa Rican border through San Juan del Sur and the entire Emerald Coast is being built, dramatically improving access and unlocking previously unreachable zones.

Full Foreign Ownership

Foreigners hold the same ownership rights as Nicaraguan citizens under Article 44 of the Constitution and the Foreign Investment Law (Ley 344). No restrictions on beachfront property via 99-year renewable concessions.

Unmatched Value vs Costa Rica

Raw land from ~$25/m². Comparable beachfront in Guanacaste, Costa Rica costs 3–5x more. The infrastructure gap is closing — the price gap won't last.

Proven Gated Communities

Rancho Santana (2,700 acres) and Hacienda Iguana offer resort-grade amenities — golf, pools, security, beachfront access — with established appreciation track records.

Record Tourism Growth

Nearly 2.1 million tourist arrivals in 2024, up 12% YoY. Featured in Travel + Leisure (2025) and Condé Nast as an emerging surf and wellness destination. Strong rental demand year-round.
